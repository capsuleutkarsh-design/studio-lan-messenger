# Quillo 1.10.0

A look-and-feel release: easier to read, quicker to find your way, and made for how a VFX studio talks -
shot names and folder paths become things you can click.

## 📍 Find your place in busy chats
- **"New messages" line.** Open a busy room and a line shows where you stopped reading (*6 new messages*).
  Quillo starts you right there, not at the bottom. Once you answer, the line goes away.
- **Jump buttons.** Scrolled up? **↓ Latest** takes you back down, and shows how many new messages came in
  meanwhile (*↓ 3 new*). If the line is above you, **↑ 6 new messages** takes you to it.
- **Pin chats to the top.** Right-click a chat → **Pin to the top**. Up to 10 chats stay in a *Pinned* group
  above the rest. Pins follow you to any PC.

## 🎬 Made for a VFX studio
- **Shot names are links.** Write `FAL_030` in a message and it turns into a link: a click shows everything said
  about that shot, in every chat you can see. The default catches `FAL_030`, `FAL_030_0010` and `SEQ010_SH0020`;
  the admin can change the pattern (*Settings → Chats → Shot names* in the server console, with a live tester).
- **Folder cards.** A path like `\\nas01\falcon\FAL_030\plates\v002` shows as a card with the folder name,
  **Open folder** and **Copy path**. A message that is only a path shows just the card.
- **Picture galleries.** Pictures sent one after another show as one gallery bubble with *Download all*.
  Click a picture and it opens **inside Quillo**, full-window: **← →** move through every picture in the chat,
  with a strip at the bottom, *Open*, *Save as* and *Copy*.

## 🔔 Fewer interruptions
- **Reply from the pop-up.** New messages pop up in the corner of the screen with a box to answer right there -
  no need to open Quillo. A reply to a thread message goes into the thread. (Settings → Notifications: switch
  back to Windows notifications if you prefer.)
- **Focus time.** Click your photo → **Focus time** → 30 minutes / 1 hour / 2 hours / until 6 PM. Only
  **@mentions**, **the person you report to** and **people you choose** get through; everything else waits
  quietly. A bar at the top shows how many are waiting, and *End now* stops it.

## 👀 Easier on the eyes
- **Text size.** Settings → Appearance → **Text size**: Small, Normal, Large, Extra large or Huge - the whole
  window, for big 4K monitors (Quillo restarts to apply it).
- **Ctrl + / Ctrl − / Ctrl 0** make the message text bigger, smaller or normal, straight away.
- **Your own messages** stand out more: on the right, in a stronger teal.
- **The chat list can be wider.** Drag the edge between the list and the chat; double-click it for the normal
  width.
- **Chat header buttons have names** on a wide window: *Buzz*, *Search*, *Calendar*, *Members*.

## 🧭 First steps and help
- **Welcome tour.** New people get four short steps on first sign-in: photo & status, find your team, your
  rooms, files and shots. Everyone sees it once after this update (*Skip* closes it for good). Open it again
  from your photo → *Welcome tour*.
- **Keyboard shortcuts.** Press **Ctrl+/** (or F1) for every shortcut in one place.
- **A tidier Home.** Cards with nothing to show (no announcements yet, no team set up) are left out, so the
  useful ones fit on one screen.
- **Friendly empty screens** with the one button you need: *Find people*, *Create a room*,
  *New announcement*, *Open the downloads folder*.

## ⬆️ Upgrading
Install over 1.9.0: **the server first, then every PC** (Updates → Publish client update, then *Install now*).
Nothing is lost.
- Pinned chats, focus time and the tour are kept on the server, so they follow people to any PC. A 1.10 client
  on a 1.9 server still works; those three are then kept only until Quillo is closed.
- A new shot name pattern reaches each PC the next time it signs in.
