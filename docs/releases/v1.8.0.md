# Quillo 1.8.0

Quillo's own stickers, a new licence with a Credits screen, and a clearer org chart.

## 🎨 459 new stickers, made for Quillo
All the stickers are new, drawn for Quillo in one cartoon style: desi characters with short Hinglish captions.

| Pack | Stickers | | Pack | Stickers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desi Slang | 30 | | Chai Time | 30 |
| Desi Slang 2 | 30 | | Filmy | 40 |
| Reactions | 30 | | Filmy Dialogues | 99 |
| Moods | 30 | | Festivals | 30 |
| Office | 40 | | Festival Icons | 60 |
| Studio Life | 40 | | | |

- **Filmy Dialogues** has its own cast (the hero, the daaku, Maa, the inspector, the seth ...) and original lines.
- **Festival Icons** has diyas, rangoli, kites, rakhis, flags, cakes and more for Indian festivals and holidays,
  without people. Some have a greeting, some are plain.
- The festival themes (Independence Day, Republic Day, Christmas) open Festival Icons in the sticker picker.
- The old 294 stickers are retired. Old sticker messages in your chat history now show
  *"Old sticker (no longer in Quillo)"*.

## 📜 New licence: UT Community Licence 2.0
Quillo stays **free**, including for paid studio and client work. In short: you may not sell it, and changes
must be sent back as a pull request. The full text is in `LICENSE.md`.
- The line at the bottom of every window reads **Quillo · © 2026 Utkarsh Tripathi · UT Community Licence 2.0**.
  Click it to open the new **Credits** screen (also in *Settings*). It lists the software Quillo is built on.
- The installers include `LICENSE.md`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and a plain `LICENSE.txt`.
- Quillo checks at startup that the licence files and the credit line are intact, and does not start when they
  are removed or changed.
- Copies up to 1.7.1 keep the Quillo Community License 1.0.

## 🌳 Org chart: a branch per department
When someone's direct reports come from **two or more departments**, each department now gets its own branch
under that person: a small label with the department name and head count, with those people below it. The
person's own department comes first and *No department* last.
- The *List* view, *By reporting line*, groups the same way.
- Teams from a single department look the same as before.

## ⬆️ Upgrading
Install over 1.7.1: the server first, then the clients. Nothing is lost.

A PC still on 1.7.1 shows *"Sticker (update Quillo to see it)"* for the new stickers until it is updated.
