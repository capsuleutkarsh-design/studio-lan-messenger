# Quillo 1.7.0

A full **studio calendar** and the whole studio's **people list in Excel**.

## 📅 The calendar
A **Calendar** button in the left rail. Month, Week, Day and Agenda views, the chosen day on the right, and
layers you switch on and off, each in its own soft colour:

| Layer | What it is | Who can add it |
|---|---|---|
| **Holidays** | The studio's holidays (see below) | Admin and HR |
| **Meetings** | Invite people, or a whole room. Invitees answer **Going / Maybe / Can't go**. Repeats: every day, **every working day (Mon–Sat)**, weekly on chosen days, monthly, until a date or N times; change or cancel **just one** occurrence. | Anyone |
| **My events** | Private appointments (dentist, pickup) | Anyone, only they see them |
| **Notes of the day** | *Only me*, *my team* (department or room) or *the whole studio* ("Fire drill at 11") | Me: anyone · team: leads and above · studio: admin, HR, management |
| **Deadlines** | "FAL delivery – Fri 18:00" for a room, a department or the studio | Production, supervisors, leads |
| **Leave** | Mark days off: the team sees you're away (not why), and your status shows **🌴 On leave** | Anyone, for themselves |
| **Birthdays** | Day and month (never the year), plus **work anniversaries** from the joining date, with *Send wishes* | From the people list |

- **Meeting reminders:** 10 minutes before by default (a notification and a sound). Each person can change or
  switch this off in *Settings → Meetings*.
- **Room meetings and deadlines** are announced in the room ("📅 Dev scheduled 'Dailies' · Mon 28 Sep, 17:00 ·
  every working day"). People invited to a meeting get a notification.
- **Home** has a small calendar (dots on busy days, holidays shaded) and what's on this week. Click a day to
  open it.
- **Each room has its own calendar**: the 📅 button in the room header shows only that room's meetings,
  deadlines and notes.
- **Import `.ics`** (*New → Import my calendar*) from Outlook, Google or Apple Calendar. Repeating events come
  along too.

### 🎉 Holidays, 2026 to 2035
India's public and festival holidays are pre-filled up to **2035**: Republic Day, Holi, Eid, Diwali, Dussehra,
Christmas and more.
- National days are already ticked. **Tick the ones your studio keeps** in the console (*Holidays*), or from the
  app (*New → Studio holidays*) for HR.
- Festival dates are worked out from the moon and sun like a panchang (Hindu festivals) and from the Hijri
  calendar (Islamic ones), and marked **check the date**. Checked against published dates for 2024–2026: 56
  of 58 match exactly. The two that differ are Holi 2026 (a lunar eclipse year) and Eid al-Fitr 2025 (moon
  sighting).
- Add your own days (studio anniversary), import an `.ics` holiday file, or **add India's list for any later
  year** (2036+).

## 📊 People list in Excel
- *Users → **Excel template***: a workbook that already lists everyone.
  - Soft Quillo colours, with the columns grouped as Person / Organisation / Calendar / Sign-in.
  - **Dropdowns** for department, section, designation and *reports to*.
  - A *How to fill* sheet.
- New columns: **Employee ID**, **Birthday**, **Joining date**. They are also in *Add/Edit user*.
- *Users → **Import*** (Excel or CSV):
  - **New usernames are created and existing people updated.** An empty cell keeps the current value, `-`
    clears it.
  - Missing departments and sections are created.
  - You first see **"X new, Y changed, Z unchanged"** plus the rows that would fail, then decide.
  - New people without a password get a random one, and you save a styled **list of first passwords** to hand
    out. They choose their own at first sign-in.

## ⬆️ Upgrading
Install over 1.6.x (server first, then clients, for example with *Updates → Publish client update*). Nothing is
lost. The database gets the calendar tables and three new person fields by itself, and the holiday list is
filled once.
