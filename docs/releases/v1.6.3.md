# Quillo 1.6.3

Buzz everywhere, @mentions for people and teams, a personal space, screenshots, a tidier sign-in screen, and the
logo takes you home.

## ✨ New

### ⚡ Buzz everywhere
- **Buzz a whole room**: the ⚡ button in a room's header shakes and rings every member's window. A room can be
  buzzed once a minute, so a big room can't be spammed. The chat shows "Ann buzzed the room".
- Buzz from anywhere: the chat header, **right-click a chat** in the list, or **right-click a person** in People
  or the org chart.
- The notification says where it came from: "BUZZ from Ann in FAL Delivery".

### @ Mentions for people and groups
Type `@` in a room and pick from:
- `@everyone`: everyone in the room
- `@here`: everyone who is online now
- `@Compositing`, `@Lighting`, `@Roto-Paint` ...: a **department or section** (shown when 2+ of its people are in
  the room)
- people, found by **name or surname** as well as username (`@kap` finds Ben Kapoor)

Mentions are highlighted in the message; the ones that mean **you** show as a chip. A mention still reaches you
in a muted room or on Do not disturb.

### 📝 My space
Your own private place for notes, to-dos, links and files:
- the **Me** button in the left rail
- always first in **Chats** as **My space**

Only you can see it.

### 📸 Screenshots
The 🖼 button in the message box (or **Ctrl+Shift+S**, or *Attach → Take a screenshot*):
1. Quillo steps out of the way.
2. **Drag** over any part of any screen, or **click** for the whole screen (**Esc** cancels).
3. A preview opens: add a message and **Send**, or **Copy** it to paste somewhere else.

### 🔐 Sign-in screen without the IP address
The sign-in screen now shows just "Server: Studio Messenger" with a **Change** link. The address field appears
only when you click *Change*, when no server can be found, or when the server doesn't answer.

### 🏠 Logo = Home
Click the Quillo logo at the top of the rail to go back to the Home screen from anywhere.

## ⬆️ Upgrading
Install over 1.6.2 (server first, then clients, for example with *Publish client update*). Nothing is lost.
