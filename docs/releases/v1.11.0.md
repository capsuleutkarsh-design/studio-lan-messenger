# Quillo 1.11.0

Review tools for a VFX studio, faster everyday chat, and a round of polish across every screen.

## 🎬 Reviews, right in the chat
- **Shot status.** Right-click a message that mentions a shot → **Set FAL_030 status**: 🛠 WIP · 🔍 Ready for
  review · 🔁 Changes · ✅ Approved · ⭐ Client approved · 🚀 Delivered. Anyone in the chat can set it; the chat
  says who did. The status then shows **next to the shot name in every message** (`FAL_030 ✅`), and clicking the
  shot shows its status on top of everything said about it, with *Set status*.
- **Draw on a picture.** In the picture viewer click ✏ (or *Draw on it* on a screenshot): pen, arrow, box,
  circle and text in five colours, then send it to the chat with a note. The paint-over notes supervisors give
  every day, without leaving Quillo.
- **Compare two versions.** In the picture viewer click ◫ (or press **C**): the next picture appears under a
  line you drag across (wipe), or **side by side**. Both versions are named on the picture.
- **EXR, DPX, TIFF and MOV previews.** Frames and dailies clips show a preview in the chat (clips with a ▶), and
  open in the viewer like any picture. The **server** makes the previews - nobody downloads a 400 MB clip just
  to see what is in it.

## ⚡ Faster every day
- **Alt+Shift+↓** jumps to the next chat with unread messages (**Alt+Shift+↑** goes back).
- **Mark every chat as read**: the ✓✓ button above your chats (it shows when something is unread).
- **Save for later**: right-click a message → *Save for later*. The 🔖 button above your chats lists them;
  double-click one to go to it. Saved messages follow you to any PC.
- **Drafts**: text you typed and didn't send shows in the chat list in red (*Draft: ...*), and is still there
  after Quillo or the PC restarts.

## 📅 Calendar and status
- **"📅 In a meeting"** shows as your status while a calendar meeting of yours runs, and clears itself when it
  ends. It never replaces a status you set yourself. (Settings → Notifications to switch it off.)
- **Automatic reply on leave**: when you add leave, tick *Answer direct messages automatically* and write the
  reply ("Back on Monday, for FAL roto ask Lea"). Everyone who writes to you gets it once a day. It doesn't
  count as an unread message for them.

## 🛠 For the admin
- **Update tracker** (console → Updates): every person, the Quillo they last signed in with, from which PC, and
  who still needs the update. **Remind them** shows the update bar again on every signed-in PC that is behind.
- **Not seen in 30 days** (console → Reports): people who haven't signed in for a month - probably left, or
  their PC is gone.

## ✨ Polish
- **Calendar week and day**: the day names (and all-day items like holidays and deadlines) now stay on top
  while you scroll the hours - before, you couldn't tell which column was which day.
- **Forms line up**: labels sit level with their boxes in every dialog and in the console's settings.
- **Time boxes** in the meeting dialog no longer show stray arrow marks; the date isn't cut off.
- **Reminders** show "19:45", not "19:45:57".
- **Check boxes in lists** (people pickers, invite lists) are clearly visible.
- **The console's warning box** showed `&#9888;&nbsp;` instead of ⚠ - fixed, and it's an amber warning now,
  not a red alarm.
- **The built-in "Administrator" account** no longer shows up as a colleague in People, the org chart, Forward
  and Search.
- **People page**: no more "+ new room" button there (it belongs to Chats and Rooms).
- **Compact view**: long system lines wrap instead of being cut off.
- **Reports chart**: slim bars and a clear "busiest day" label, instead of one wall of colour.

## ⬆️ Upgrading
Install over 1.10.0: **the server first, then every PC** (console → Updates → Publish client update, then
*Install now* - or **Remind them**). Nothing is lost.
- The server installer is bigger (about 30 MB more): it brings FFmpeg for the EXR/MOV previews. The client
  installer stays the same size.
- FFmpeg is a separate program under the GPL licence; its note is in `FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt` next to the server.
