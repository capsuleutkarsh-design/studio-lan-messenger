# Quillo 1.12.1

A new compact view, and leads can look at their team's screens without waiting.

## 📱 A new compact view
- **Tabs along the bottom**, like a phone app: Chats, People, Calendar, Files and **More**. The left rail is gone
  in compact view, so the chat gets the whole width. Unread counts show on the tabs.
- **More** has everything else in one menu: your status and profile photo, Home, Rooms, Org chart, News (its
  unread count shows on More), My space, Search, Saved, Focus time, reminders, *Keep on top*, light / dark,
  Settings and Sign out.
- **The chat's name shows in full.** The header keeps only Back, the name and ⋯. Buzz and Room members are
  in ⋯, and clicking the room's name or picture opens its members.
- **Smaller avatars** in the messages, so the bubbles get more of the width.
- **A softer look:** the pinned message is a rounded card, and there's no line under the header.
- **⤢ Full window** above the chat list and in the chat header takes you back to the normal window (Ctrl+Shift+M
  still works too).
- **Settings → Compact view:** choose the width (Narrow, Normal, Wide) and whether it docks on the **right or
  left** edge of the screen.

## 🖥 Screen sharing for leads
- **Admins and leads see a screen straight away.** Anyone above a person in the org chart (their lead, the lead's
  lead...) and admins click **See their screen** and it opens, without waiting for an answer.
- **The person always knows:** they get a notification ("👀 Rajiv Menon is viewing your screen") and the red bar
  at the top of their screen says who is watching. **Stop sharing** on the bar ends it.
- **The console's audit log** records every time ("viewed a screen without asking").
- **Everyone else still asks** as before, and anyone can still offer to share their own screen.
- It follows the reporting lines set in the console (Users → Reports to). Please let your team know that
  their leads can view their screen this way.

## ⬆️ Upgrading
Install over 1.12.0 (or older): **the server first, then every PC** (console → Updates → Publish client update,
then *Install now* - or **Remind them**). Nothing is lost. Viewing without asking needs the 1.12.1 server.
